Why Maintenance Matters

Stop Breakdowns Before They Happen.

Your HVAC system is one of the most expensive investments in your home. Just like a car, it requires regular tune-ups to run smoothly. Skipping maintenance leads to restricted airflow, strained motors, and eventually, total system failure.

  • Lower Energy Bills

    A clean, well-lubricated system uses significantly less electricity to cool your home, keeping your monthly utility costs down.

  • Extend System Lifespan

    Regular servicing prevents minor wear-and-tear from turning into catastrophic damage, adding years to your unit's life.

  • Improve Indoor Air Quality

    We clear out dust, mold, and debris from your coils and drain lines, ensuring the air circulating in your home is safe and clean.

Our 21-Point Tune-Up Checklist

We leave no stone unturned. Every maintenance visit includes a thorough inspection, cleaning, and testing of your entire system.

Electrical & Controls

  • Inspect & tighten all wiring connections
  • Test capacitors & relays
  • Measure voltage & amperage on motors
  • Calibrate thermostat settings
  • Test safety controls and limit switches

Cooling & Airflow

  • Measure temperature drop across coils
  • Inspect & clean evaporator coil
  • Inspect & clean condenser coil
  • Check refrigerant levels & subcooling
  • Replace or clean air filters (if subscribed)

Mechanical & Drainage

  • Flush and clear condensate drain line
  • Inspect drain pan for rust/overflow
  • Lubricate all moving parts & bearings
  • Inspect fan motor and blower assembly
  • Check ductwork for visible leaks
