Frequently asked questions

quick answers to common HVAC concerns
How does your filter delivery subscription work?
We make it hassle-free! You tell us your exact filter sizes and preferred MERV rating. We'll automatically deliver premium filters to your doorstep right when it's time to change them (typically every 30, 60, or 90 days). Shipping is always free.
Do you offer emergency AC repair?
Absolutely. We understand that AC failures don't wait for business hours. Our mobile technicians are on standby 24/7 for emergency repairs. Most dispatch calls are handled within a 2-hour window.
Are your technicians certified?
Yes, all our technicians are fully licensed, insured, and thoroughly background-checked. We require our team to undergo continuous training to stay updated on the latest residential and commercial HVAC technologies.
