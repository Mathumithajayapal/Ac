Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our automated filter deliveries, AC maintenance, billing, and emergency repair services.

Filter Delivery Subscriptions

How do I know what size filter I need?
The easiest way is to check the cardboard edge of your current air filter. You'll typically see three numbers representing Length x Width x Depth (e.g., 20x25x1). If you cannot find it, you can measure the filter slot in your HVAC unit or schedule a consultation, and our techs will determine the perfect fit.
How often will you deliver my filters?
You can customize your delivery frequency to every 30, 60, or 90 days. We recommend 30 days for homes with multiple pets or allergy sufferers, 60 days for standard households, and 90 days for single-occupant homes without pets.
Can I skip a delivery or pause my subscription?
Absolutely. You have total control over your subscription through the Customer Dashboard. You can easily skip a shipment, adjust your delivery frequency, or cancel entirely without any hidden fees or penalties.

AC Maintenance & Repair

Do you offer emergency AC repair?
Yes, we understand that AC failures don't wait for business hours. Our mobile technicians are on standby 24/7 for emergency repairs. Most dispatch calls are handled within a 2-hour window to get your home cooling again quickly.
What is included in routine maintenance?
Our routine tune-up includes a comprehensive 21-point inspection. We clean the condenser coils, check refrigerant levels, inspect electrical connections, clear the condensate drain line, test thermostat functionality, and ensure your system is running at peak efficiency.
Are your technicians certified?
Yes, all our technicians are fully licensed, insured, and thoroughly background-checked. We require our team to undergo continuous training to stay updated on the latest residential and commercial HVAC technologies.

Billing & Accounts

When will I be billed for my filter subscription?
You are only billed when your filters actually ship. If you are on a 60-day schedule, you will see a charge on your designated payment method every 60 days exactly on the shipping date.
Do you offer commercial maintenance plans?
Yes. We partner with offices, retail stores, and apartment complexes to provide bulk filter delivery and scheduled HVAC maintenance. Please reach out to our commercial team for tiered pricing and custom service plans.
