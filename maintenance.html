Preventative Care

Professional AC Maintenance & Tune-Ups

Keep your air conditioning system running efficiently, lower your energy bills, and extend the lifespan of your unit with our comprehensive maintenance services.

Why Regular Maintenance Matters

Skipping routine maintenance can lead to costly repairs and poor air quality. Here’s why a healthy AC is a smart investment.

Maximize Efficiency

A clean, well-lubricated system uses significantly less energy to cool your home, lowering your monthly utility bills.

Extend Equipment Life

Routine check-ups reduce strain on critical components, helping your AC unit last years longer before needing replacement.

Prevent Breakdowns

We catch and resolve minor issues before they become major, expensive emergencies during the hottest days of the year.

Our Comprehensive 21-Point Tune-Up

Every AeroHVAC maintenance visit includes a thorough inspection, cleaning, and calibration of your entire cooling system to ensure it operates flawlessly.

  • Clean & inspect condenser coils
  • Check refrigerant charge levels
  • Calibrate thermostat settings
  • Inspect electrical connections
  • Clear condensate drain line
  • Lubricate moving parts
  • Test safety controls
  • Replace standard air filter
